## About Me

I am a Computer Engineering student who learns best by building, breaking things carefully, and understanding what happens beneath the surface.

My current path sits at the intersection of **agentic engineering**, **software development**, **Linux**, and **cybersecurity**. I am interested not only in using tools, but also in understanding how they work, how they fail, how they can be improved, and how reliable systems can be built around them.

I treat every project as both a product and a lesson. The goal is not to collect technologies. The goal is to develop the judgment required to design, build, inspect, and eventually secure real systems.

## What I’m Learning

### Agentic Engineering

I am learning how AI agents can work with tools, structured instructions, context, memory, and reusable workflows. My focus is gradually moving from simply prompting models toward understanding how dependable agentic systems are designed.

### Software Development

I am strengthening my fundamentals through practical projects and coursework, with experience across **C**, **Java**, **JavaScript**, Git, GitHub, and web-based development.

### Linux

I use **Zorin OS** as my main environment and continue improving my command-line habits, Bash knowledge, system understanding, and confidence working outside graphical interfaces.

### Cybersecurity

I am developing a security-oriented way of thinking: identifying trust boundaries, questioning assumptions, understanding attack surfaces, and learning how systems can be made more resilient.
